Open Seat, five ways

Five visual directions for Open Seat, pulled from my mood board. Each one shows the same three screens: a quiz question, the board, and a group. Every quiz screen shows the picked answer staying highlighted, since V3 testing showed nobody could tell what they'd tapped.

The archetype mascots are rough placeholders I drew to show how characters could work in each style. They're not final branding.

Proof of concept for picking a direction as a group. Nothing here is wired to the live app yet. Pick one (or mix: one direction's type with another's color) and we'll restyle V3 to match.
