# Open Seat V3: plan

V3 builds on [V2](../Loudon/Open_Seat_V2/) using what we heard from my 5 user tests ([notes](../notes/Loudon_V1_User_Testing_Notes.md)) and the ideas from class crit.

## Big idea: a personality quiz instead of a profile

Instead of making people fill out a form, the first thing you do is a quick, fun personality quiz (about 6 taps). It's less invasive and less of a chore, but it still gives the app what it needs:

- your **archetype** (your emoji and a short label everyone at the table sees)
- your **comfort settings** (who you're OK sitting with)
- the **logistics** (how much time you have, dietary needs, budget)
- your agreement to the **"Open Seat is for everyone"** rules

## Comfort settings: how matching works

Everyone's settings have to work for **everyone** at the table, both ways:

- **Anyone:** no limits.
- **Women and nonbinary people only:** you're only matched into groups where everyone else is a woman or nonbinary.
- **Mixed, up to N men:** you're only matched into groups with at most N men besides you.
- People who chose "Prefer not to say" can still join tables without limits. Tables with limits only include people who shared their gender, so "Prefer not to say" counts like a man toward a limit.

Where it applies:

- **Groups you can join:** you only see groups whose members' settings fit with yours, and yours with theirs.
- **Spin:** only lands you in groups that fit.
- **Random groups:** a group is built from people in line whose settings all fit together.

Nobody sees anyone else's gender or comfort settings. They only see the groups that fit them.

## Keeping out hateful people (honestly)

A quiz can't catch someone who's homophobic or hateful, because people can just click "I'm in." So safety comes from several layers working together:

1. **Comfort settings**, so you only meet the kinds of people you said you're OK with.
2. **The "Open Seat is for everyone" rules**: every gender, sexuality, race and religion is welcome, and disrespect gets you removed.
3. **Block and report** *(next)*.
4. **A private check after each meetup** *(next)*: "Did you feel safe?" and "Would you meet again?" No public ratings. Someone who keeps getting flagged stops getting matched.

## What V3 builds now

- The quiz (replaces the emoji picker), with a "Retake quiz" button.
- Archetype emoji and label, plus dietary/budget tags, shown for everyone at a table. This answers user 5 ("only the host describes themselves").
- Comfort-based matching for Join, Spin and random groups.
- A 15-minute option for groups.
