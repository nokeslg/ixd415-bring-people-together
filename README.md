## Who we might be designing for, and why

Week 1 homework: one paragraph each. Rewrite this section whenever the project changes.

### Loudon

Upon getting the project brief, my immediate thought went to two different areas. One of these areas was a party game that could stay relevant for years to come. So many party games that I play tend to feel dated even a few years later because they fail to update and rely on meta humor. The second area was overall social connection and micro-event based interactions. Young adults seem to feel more lonely than ever and rather than direct people to events that already exist, I think that creating a platform to help organize small events in your area could be a fun way to meet people while bonding over something you're all passionate about. Either way, it seems that young adults are my main target audience simply because I think that I can relate to them and also because they are seemingly struggling the most in today's current world.

### Cami

I think we’ll end up designing for college students and young adults who want to meet more people their age or feel part of a stronger community. Many already want more connection, but busy schedules and the awkwardness of approaching strangers get in the way. Our platform would lower that barrier by making it easy to meet new people spontaneously.

### Madi

I think our target audience will be college students with busy schedules, who have a hard time branching out and making friends, or keeping in contact with their current friends who have conflicting schedules. Something digital like a shared game or a calendar that forces you to spend time with each other could potentially solve these issues.

## The brief, in short

Design something that gets real people working in a product together, online and maybe also in real life, and test it with at least 100 unique people.

- **100 testers, minimum.** Tally in [`numbers.md`](numbers.md).
- **5 people in live interaction at the same time** through the final version. Five at once, not five over a week.
- **Synchronous multiplayer.** It has to support 5 people at once, with synchronous activity and visibility.
- **A software component.** It doesn't have to be an app: a website, a chatbot, SMS, or a Discord bot all count. Any real-life part must also involve the software.
- **Test with real people every week.** No exceptions. If it isn't testable in a week, scope down.
- **Safety.** Basic guardrails against destructive behavior.
- **Document the interactions** with video, screen recordings, chat logs and photos.
